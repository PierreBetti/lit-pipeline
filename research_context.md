## Project

PhD in biology (UQAM). Carbon and greenhouse gas dynamics (CO2, CH4, N2O) in
forested wetlands, mainly forested swamps, of southern Québec, with the aim of
informing nature-based climate solutions.

Fluxes are measured with closed chambers on soil collars and on tree stems
(LI-COR LI-7810 / LI-7820 trace gas analyzers). No aquatic (water surface)
flux measurements.

## Planned chapters

1. Flux budget and microclimate controls (soil and stem fluxes, temperature,
   water table, seasonal and spatial variability).
2. Vegetation: role of trees and understory, stem fluxes, plant-mediated transport.
3. Soil and water microbial communities: taxonomic and functional diversity,
   methanogens, methanotrophs, nitrifiers / denitrifiers.
4. Hydrology, biogeochemistry and contaminants (water chemistry, DOC, nutrients,
   redox, anthropogenic inputs).

## Research questions

- RQ1: What controls whether a southern Québec forested swamp is a net GHG source or sink?
- RQ2: How does a disturbed forested wetland compare to an intact one?
- RQ3: How is anthropogenic impact expressed in forested wetlands?
- RQ4: What is the distribution of carbon sources between soil and water in wetlands?
- RQ5: What is the role of taxonomic and functional microbial diversity in the
  belowground systems of forested wetlands in southern Québec?
