      Where the science is, from broad to specific

      Each ring is a scope of the literature, from all wetlands (outside) to forested swamps (inside); your library is the core. Each sector is one of your research questions, shaded by the share of that scope's papers addressing it. Hatched sectors are gaps. Click a sector for numbers and confidence intervals.

      Papers are classified a batch at a time each night, innermost scope first. Large scopes are represented by a fixed random sample, so their numbers are estimates.

        Share of the field's papers vs. share of your library, per period. A period where your share is much lower than the field's may be underrepresented in your reading.
